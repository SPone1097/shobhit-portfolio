Recruiting

Finding, assessing and onboarding people, from outbound sourcing to a screening pipeline that keeps a recruiter in charge of the decision. Targeting Recruiter and Talent Sourcer roles in Berlin and across the EU.

Full-Cycle Recruiting Boolean Sourcing Outbound Outreach Interviewing & Negotiation Onboarding Screening Automation ATS (Google Sheets)

Highlights

01

Sourced and hired a four-person remote team from scratch, outbound only with no job postings. Candidates were found with Boolean search on LinkedIn, and a second hiring batch came through referrals.

02

Ran the full hiring cycle: outreach, first call, interview and negotiation, with offers issued through a colleague.

03

Chose hiring markets by talent-to-cost ratio using AI-assisted search and market research, and hired from the Philippines on two full-time and two freelance contracts.

04

Checked every hire against budget and team fit, including demographics and time zones. Two of the four stayed on after I left the team.

05

Onboarded every hire personally, from the first-week plan to the point they worked independently.

06

As an SME at Amazon Pune, sat on the recruitment team: advised interviewing managers on which questions to ask and what to look for, and owned new-hire onboarding, including the first-month plan and the criteria for handling calls, chats and emails independently. Trained 15 new hires, 14 of whom passed (93%).

07

Built an n8n candidate screening pipeline with Claude: Gemini scores CVs 1 to 5 against a job rubric, a Google Sheet works as the ATS, strong candidates get an interview invite, and every rejection is held 48 hours for a recruiter to review. See the build.

08

Designed the pipeline around human review of rejections (GDPR Art. 22 and the EU AI Act): the AI reads and sorts, a recruiter decides. Tested it on tricky CVs, including one with hidden prompt text and one unreadable scan.
