Strategic Marketing — MAHLE

A solo case study on automotive supplier MAHLE — history, financials, global footprint, competitive position, and its path to zero-carbon powertrains.

What this was

A full case study on MAHLE, a leading automotive components supplier, built solo end to end — from company history through to strategic recommendations on where the business is headed.

  • Traced MAHLE's history from its 1920 founding through its 2019 chargeBIG start-up launch.
  • Broke down the business overview and financials — €10.9B in 2021 sales across five business units, plus R&D spend and regional sales mix.
  • Mapped its global footprint across roughly 60 countries and its competitive position against Schaeffler, Valeo, Hitachi, and Kongsberg Automotive.
  • Closed with MAHLE's roadmap to zero-carbon powertrains — electricity, hydrogen, non-fossil fuels, and gas as the four transition paths.

    • Why it's here

    It's the clearest solo evidence of building a complete company analysis from public sources — pulling financials, competitive data, and strategic direction into one coherent narrative without a team to divide the work.
