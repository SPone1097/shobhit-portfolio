Pouch Cell Factory Simulation

A ground-up factory layout for lithium pouch cell production, built as project lead for a five-person MBA team using Siemens Technomatix.

Role
Project Manager
Team
5 members
Tools
Technomatix, Overleaf
Output
2 reports (24 & 98 pages)

What this was

An MBA coursework project simulating the setup of a pouch cell battery factory from a blank floor plan. The brief called for a working layout, a realistic project plan, and a report documenting both the technical and management sides of the build.

  • Built the full factory layout in Siemens Technomatix, sequencing production stages from raw material intake to finished cell output.
  • Led a 5-person cross-functional team, splitting workstreams across layout design, capacity planning, and reporting.
  • Tracked progress with a Work Breakdown Structure and Gantt chart, keeping the team aligned against a fixed academic deadline.
  • Produced weekly operational and management reports in Overleaf, culminating in two final reports: a 24-page summary and a 98-page full report.

Why it's here

It's the clearest evidence of end-to-end ownership on file: scoping a build with no existing template, coordinating a team against a deadline, and documenting the outcome to a professional standard. The same instincts show up later in building the Reinstatement team at Profitzon from scratch.
