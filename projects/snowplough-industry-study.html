Snowplough Industry Study

A market research deep-dive into the global snowplough industry, from its 19th-century origins to today's autonomous equipment.

Format
Team project
Scope
Global market
Market size
$2.8B, 3% CAGR
Output
15-slide report

What this was

A market research project tracing an unglamorous but genuinely global industry (snow removal) from horse-drawn carts and hand shovels through to autonomous airport snowploughs.

  • Traced the industry's history, from 1862 horse-drawn snow rollers to the first car-mounted plough in 1923.
  • Mapped market segmentation and key suppliers (Meyer Products, Western Products, SnowDogg, Fisher Engineering) across residential, commercial, and public-sector customers.
  • Sized the market (public snow removal in the US alone ran $3-4B in 2016) and flagged the operational risks and supply-chain pressures facing contractors.
  • Covered emerging autonomous technology (Yeti Move's airport snowploughs and Snowbot, the first residential autonomous snowblower) plus substitutes like heated roads.

    • Why it's here

    It shows the same research instinct applied somewhere with zero prior familiarity: build a full picture of an unfamiliar industry from public sources, then turn it into findings someone could actually act on.
