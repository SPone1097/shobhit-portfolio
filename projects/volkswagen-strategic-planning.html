Strategic Business Planning for Volkswagen

A post-2038 corporate strategy for Volkswagen's brand portfolio and EV transition, built with no prior framework to follow.

Format
Team project
Frameworks
SWOT, Importance/Urgency Matrix
Course
Business Operations Management
Output
7 recommendations

What this was

A strategy project asking: what should Volkswagen's brand portfolio and market position look like heading toward 2038, when the EU's combustion-engine phase-out lands? No template was given, the brief was open-ended, so the first task was picking a structure that could actually carry the analysis.

  • Ran a full SWOT analysis across Volkswagen's brand portfolio, from global presence and financial stability to slow reaction times on emerging trends like Mobility-as-a-Service.
  • Cross-matched every SWOT pairing into concrete moves (where to invest, where to pull back, what to build), then scored each one on an Importance/Urgency Matrix to separate immediate actions from 5-10 year bets.
  • Landed on seven final recommendations spanning brand investment (more into SCANIA and MAN, reassessed strategy for AUDI and BUGATTI), reducing Chinese market dependency, and building out a dedicated public-transportation segment.

    • Why it's here

    It's a case study in building structure from nothing: no rubric, no past example to copy, just a two-tool framework built to make a genuinely open question decidable.
